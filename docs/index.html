Three planes, one die

The planes are bonded by Cu-Cu thermocompression at 200 nm pitch and operated at about 1 K.

PLANE 1

QCPP: Quantum Coherent Processing Plane

28Si spin qubit arrays. Each synapse is a k-qubit superposition protected by a distance-3 surface code.

Cu-Cu bond · 200 nm pitch
PLANE 2

SSP: Spintronic Synaptic Plane

PMA-MTJ memristors with four resistance states (2 bits) and 0.1 fJ spin-orbit-torque switching.

Cu-Cu bond · 200 nm pitch
PLANE 3

CPP: CMOS Peripheral Plane

3 nm cryo-CMOS FinFETs for LIF neurons, the MWPM syndrome decoder and SerDes links.

Contributions

What is proposed, and what the code checks.

  1. Three-plane monolithic architectureQuantum, spintronic and CMOS layers integrated vertically instead of linked across a cryostat.
  2. Quantum synaptic encodingA synapse is a distribution over 2k weight states, which gives Bayesian uncertainty with no extra sampling hardware.
  3. Q-STDP learning ruleSpike-timing plasticity in the amplitude domain. Potentiation shifts probability toward higher weights and depression toward lower ones.
  4. Coherence budgetd = 3 gives a 10x gain at 0.1% error but none at the ~1% measured above 1 K. Holding amplitudes for a 10 ms window is out of reach today; re-preparing the state on each spike needs ~1.9 µs of gates, plus initialization and readout.
  5. Energy with cooling11.1 fJ per event at the device, at least 3.3 pJ at the wall plug once the Carnot cost of 1 K operation is charged.
  6. Hardware security analysisSide channels, learning-rule poisoning and cryogenic Trojans, plus candidate PUF and random-number primitives.

Security

Quantum-neuromorphic hardware inherits threats from both of its parents. The threat model covers four areas.

Side channels

Timing fingerprinting of cloud quantum hardware in 10 queries (GLSVLSI 2025) and up to 85.7% accuracy identifying a victim's algorithm via crosstalk (NDSS 2025).

Adversarial weights

Sampling makes inference stochastic, but stochastic defenses can be bypassed by gradient averaging, so no robustness is claimed without testing. Q-STDP opens a training-time poisoning channel.

On-chip primitives

Quantum-dot PUFs from charge noise and MTJ random numbers. Thermal switching is suppressed at 1 K, so random-number cells need near-critical biasing. Crossbar PQC acceleration is a research direction only.

Supply chain

Trusted-foundry flow and post-fabrication verification at 1 K for cryo-CMOS logic locking.

Run it

Python 3.10 or newer, with numpy and matplotlib. It runs in a few seconds.

git clone https://github.com/sunilgentyala/QNHS-Research-2026.git
cd QNHS-Research-2026
pip install -r requirements.txt

python run_all_simulations.py    # models and 102 tests, figures/*.png
python -m pytest tests/ -v
